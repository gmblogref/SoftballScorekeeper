# Softball Scorekeeper v2.0.1

## Release highlights

- Reorder the persisted live batting lineup during an active scoring session from either View Lineup or Manage Live Lineup.
- Drag Players into a new sequence, cancel without changing the Game, or save the order while keeping the current numeric batting slot fixed. Current Batter, On Deck, In The Hole, and wrap-around then resolve from the saved sequence.
- Preserve saved reorders across Exit/Resume and include each reorder as its own chronological scoring Undo/Redo action without changing earlier plays, score, outs, inning, frozen starting-lineup history, or completed plate appearances.
- Prevent reordering while a scoring play or opponent half-inning transition is unresolved, or after the scoring session or Team Year is no longer editable.

## Windows usage

Extract the entire portable ZIP to a writable folder, keep its application and
licensing files together, and run `SoftballScorekeeper.exe`. No installer is included.

## Known limitations

- Physical acceptance of the live-lineup reorder touch workflow on the target 10-inch Android tablet remains deferred; dedicated Android emulator acceptance and user-performed Windows manual acceptance passed.
- Reordering is intentionally unavailable while a play or opponent half-inning transition is pending and for completed, missing, or finalized scoring sessions.
- The Windows portable executable is unsigned and may display a Windows security warning.
- The documented Microsoft downstream-license acceptance question for the Windows portable distribution remains unresolved; no legal clearance is claimed.
